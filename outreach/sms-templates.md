# Cold SMS templates

First-touch and follow-up texts for independent companions in Canada. The pitch is a private website: screening and etiquette in one place, tour dates, and a domain they own. The tone is a peer who knows the booking line is for clients.

These are commercial electronic messages under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Polite wording does not create consent. Send one only after the lead passes [casl-checklist.md](casl-checklist.md).

Every send, including both follow-ups, keeps three things in the message:

1. Your name and business name, written the same way they appear on your contact page.
2. `{{PortfolioLink}}`, which opens a page with your Canadian mailing address. No login.
3. `Reply STOP to opt out.`

Write for a lock screen. No comments on photos or appearance, no explicit language, and no emoji. Emoji and curly quotes switch the text to Unicode, which cuts a segment from 160 characters to 70. Straight apostrophes only.

Use one angle per person. Do not send all four.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Replace with |
| --- | --- |
| `{{FirstName}}` | The name they use publicly. Skip the text if you only have a stage initial and are guessing. |
| `{{City}}` | The city on the listing you reviewed. |
| `{{AdPlatform}}` | The board they actually use, such as LeoList. |
| `{{PortfolioLink}}` | Your contact page, with your legal name and a Canadian street address or PO box. |
| `{{MyName}}` | Your name. |
| `{{BusinessName}}` | The name you carry on business. |

`{{MyName}}`, `{{BusinessName}}`, and `{{PortfolioLink}}` are still open. Do not send until those are real and the page at the link shows the mailing address.

## Which angle

| Template | Send it when |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Screening and etiquette | Their ad already spends space on rules, deposits, or "read this first." |
| 2. Editorial and tours | They tour, or they list several cities. |
| 3. Platform independence | One board is the only place clients can find them. |
| 4. Private preview | You are unsure, or they already look established and private. |

## 1. Screening and etiquette

A single link that holds rules, screening, and deposits, so fewer unfinished conversations reach the phone.

```text
{{FirstName}}, I'll keep this short. Your line is for bookings. I'm {{MyName}} at {{BusinessName}}. I build private sites so etiquette, screening, and deposits are handled before a new client texts you. {{PortfolioLink}} Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## 2. Editorial and tours

A quieter storefront for rates, city dates, and text-only booking, for someone who does not want the board to be the whole brand.

```text
{{FirstName}}, I'm {{MyName}} at {{BusinessName}}. I design quiet editorial sites for independents who tour: rates, city dates, and text-only booking, so {{AdPlatform}} is not your only storefront. {{PortfolioLink}} Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## 3. Platform independence

The client list survives a removed ad or a board outage because the domain is theirs.

```text
{{FirstName}}, if {{AdPlatform}} goes down, your regulars still need a door you own. I'm {{MyName}} ({{BusinessName}}). A fast private site on your own domain. {{PortfolioLink}} Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## 4. Private preview

A one-page preview, only if they want it. Do not build that preview from their ad photos unless they ask. Those photos are theirs.

```text
{{FirstName}}, {{MyName}} at {{BusinessName}}. If you want, I will send a private one-page preview for a discreet {{City}} site. Looking is free. Samples: {{PortfolioLink}} Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Follow-up 1 — day 3

Send on calendar day 3 only if there has been no reply, no opt-out, and the listing still passes the checklist that morning. One thread, not a new angle.

```text
{{FirstName}}, {{MyName}} at {{BusinessName}} again. If a private site for screening and tour dates would help, samples are here: {{PortfolioLink}}. If the timing is off, ignore this. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Follow-up 2 — day 7, last text

Send on calendar day 7 under the same conditions. This ends the sequence.

```text
{{FirstName}}, last note from me. {{MyName}}, {{BusinessName}}. I will not text again. If you ever want a site you own: {{PortfolioLink}} Reply STOP to opt out.
```

If they reply at any point, leave the sequence. Answer a question if they ask one. If the reply is a no, stop that day and do not send the breakup text later.

## Character budget

Each template is under 320 characters before you substitute anything, which is two GSM-7 segments. Recount the filled message anyway. A long business name plus a long URL can eat the margin.

Count check with Avery, Toronto, LeoList, `https://yoursite.ca/contact`, Alex Chen, and Northline. That sample is for length only. Do not send it.

| Template | Characters with that sample |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Screening and etiquette | 237 |
| 2. Editorial and tours | 225 |
| 3. Platform independence | 187 |
| 4. Private preview | 188 |
| Follow-up 1 | 200 |
| Follow-up 2 | 154 |

If a real fill goes past 320, shorten the pitch sentence. Keep the name, the business name, the link, and `Reply STOP to opt out.`

## Guardrails

- Send in the late morning or early afternoon in their time zone. A booking line may be in an appointment in the evening.
- The link has to resolve. The page shows your name, your business name, and a Canadian mailing address without a login.
- Honour STOP and any plain refusal (`no thanks`, `remove me`, `do not text`). Same day. The legal outside limit is 10 business days. Do not use it.
- Do not restart from a second number after they refuse or after the day-7 text.
- Do not mention their body, their photos, or their rates.
- Do not attach a mockup built from their images unless they ask for it.
