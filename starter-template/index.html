Private Inquiries

How do I book?

Send a text with your first name, the date, how long you would like, and incall or outcall. A sentence about the evening is welcome. Please send a text. Voice calls are not answered.

Do you require screening?

If we have not met, I screen before I share an address or agree to a lobby. A reference from another independent, or a deposit, is how a first meeting is confirmed.

How do deposits work?

A deposit holds the hour. It is part of the rate, not a fee on top, and it is arranged by text before the time is confirmed. A missed appointment or a late cancellation keeps the deposit.

Incall or outcall?

Both. Incall is downtown. Outcall adds $150, and we meet in the lobby first. Say which you prefer in the opening text.

What should I know before we meet?

Arrive clean. Be sober enough to talk and to consent. Phones stay away during the time you booked. The rate we confirmed by text is the rate.

How discreet are you?

Names, hotels, and conversations stay private. I expect the same care in return. I meet adults who are 18 or older, and I decline pressure for an address before screening is finished.
